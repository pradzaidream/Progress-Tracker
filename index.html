About the goal line

2 lb a week from 228 lb puts you at 185 lb on Friday 29 January 2027 — sixty-one days after the race. That rate sits at the fast end of what people hold without giving up strength or top-end speed, so treat it as a ceiling rather than a floor. The green band shows 1.5 to 2 lb a week, and anywhere inside it is a good week.

One thing to plan around: a deep deficit through October will cost you sled and wall-ball output on 20 November. You'll be around 205 lb on the 2 lb line by race day. If the sessions start feeling flat, ease to the slow edge of the band through the taper and make it back up in December and January.
